Overview

Voices Unmuted VM is the digital home for a survivor-led movement. The narrative contrasts promised refuge with lived violence at Vista Maria (a Detroit residential treatment facility), centers named survivor stories, draws parallels to other facilities, and channels visitors into a petition to reform Michigan’s civil statute of limitations — which can block many CSA suits after age 28.

What the site covers

  • Survivor stories — Meg, Elena, Mitchelle, Christian, Nikkie, Ashley, Hillary, Janene
  • Context that the residential program closed Dec 2025 while advocacy continues
  • Campaign pillars: amplify, expose, educate, reform law
  • Petition CTA for Michigan civil statute-of-limitations reform
