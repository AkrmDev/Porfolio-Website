Challenge

Creators and small teams juggle Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, and YouTube with fragmented tools for login, scheduling, captions, and analytics. The goal was one coherent product for planning and publishing without leaving the app.

Solution

Built a React frontend with Firebase auth and storage, plus Node/Express integrations so users can schedule posts weeks ahead, auto-publish, generate AI captions per platform, review unified analytics, and one-click publish across six networks.

Outcomes

6Platforms in one dashboard
10k+Posts published
500+Creators
~3 minTo post across networks
