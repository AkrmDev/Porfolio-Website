Voices Unmuted

A website for a survivor-led advocacy group. It explains what happened at a Detroit residential treatment facility, shares personal stories, and asks visitors to support changing Michigan law so more survivors can seek justice.

  • Webflow
  • CMS

Overview

The organization needed a clear, respectful place online to tell a hard story and turn attention into action. I built the site in Webflow with a calm layout that puts people and next steps first.

What visitors can do on the site

  • Read what the movement is about in plain language
  • Hear from survivors through individual story pages
  • Understand why current time limits in Michigan law make many cases hard to bring
  • Sign or share a petition calling for legal reform

Deliverables

  • Custom Webflow layout with a clear reading path
  • Editable sections for stories and campaign updates
  • Live site at voicesunmutedvm.com
