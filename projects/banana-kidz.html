Overview

Banana Kidz is a brick-and-mortar toy store run by kids, for kids. The live site announces open hours, highlights Play with purpose (Learn / Lead / Grow — Mini Managers and real business skills), showcases toys and games, and points visitors to the storefront at 521 West Main St, Milan, MI 48160.

What the site covers

  • Countdown / open announcement for the grand opening
  • Play with purpose — Learn, Lead, Grow messaging for Mini Managers
  • Toys, games & more product storytelling
  • Visit details — hours (Wed–Fri 12–8, Sat 10–8, Sun 12–5; Mon–Tue closed) and phone (734) 262-7748

Deliverables

  • Brand-forward responsive layouts in Webflow CMS
  • Reusable sections for hours, visit, and program storytelling
  • Production launch on Webflow hosting at banana-kidz.com
