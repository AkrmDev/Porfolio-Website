Overview

The show needed a home base that feels bold and easy to browse — not a generic podcast template. I built a Webflow site that introduces the host, shows the episode library, and makes watching or reaching out straightforward.

What visitors can do on the site

  • Understand what the podcast is about in a few seconds
  • Browse episodes and see who appeared as a guest
  • Watch episode video embeds without leaving the site
  • Find contact info for bookings, press, or questions

Deliverables

  • Responsive marketing and content pages
  • Editable structure for new episodes and guests
  • Live site at mtjlpodcast.com
