Overview

After the split, teams needed side-by-side clarity on how each site performed for different audiences. I analyzed daily metrics — visitors, pageviews, sessions, bounce, session length — including flags for Awards Week and Awards Night.

What I built

  • Cleaning and comparison pipelines in Pandas/NumPy
  • Interactive Plotly/Dash views of engagement and bounce patterns
  • Benchmark framing against the American Music Awards site
  • Plain-language takeaways for product/UX follow-up

Stack

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • NumPy
  • Plotly
  • Dash

Outcome

In the April–June 2023 window reviewed: Grammy.com showed higher traffic and lower bounce; AMA showed stronger pages/session and duration. Directional recommendations: deepen Grammy sessions; grow AMA traffic. Findings are scoped to that timeframe, not a full-year audit.
