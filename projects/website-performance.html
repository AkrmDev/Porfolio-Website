Analytics · Mar 2024 – Apr 2024

Website Performance Analysis

A data project for The Recording Academy after they split Grammy.com and recordingacademy.com into two sites for different audiences — so the team could see how each site performed, how people engaged, and how that compared with the American Music Awards site.

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • Plotly
  • Dash
  • NumPy

Overview

When one organization runs two websites, it is hard to tell which is doing well without looking at the numbers side by side. I pulled daily web analytics for both Recording Academy sites and built charts that show visitors, pageviews, sessions, bounce rate, and average time on site — including special flags for Awards Week and Awards Night.

What this analysis covers

  • How Grammy.com and recordingacademy.com each performed after the split
  • Engagement trends over time (who stays, who leaves quickly)
  • Traffic and behavior around major marketing moments — Awards Week and Awards Night
  • A simple benchmark against the American Music Awards (AMA) competitor site

Key takeaways

In the April–June 2023 window reviewed for the project:

  • The Grammy site drew more traffic and had a lower bounce rate
  • The AMA site did better on pages per session and how long people stayed
  • Practical next step for Grammy.com: improve how long visits last and how many pages people open
  • Practical next step for AMA: grow overall traffic

These findings are directional for that timeframe — not a full-year audit of either brand.

How it was built

Python with Pandas/NumPy for cleaning and comparing the daily metrics, and Plotly/Dash to turn the results into interactive charts stakeholders could explore.
